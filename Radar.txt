Data Structures and Algorithms Project: Anti-aircraft autocannon Radar software based simulation

Task
1)Radar Section:
i)The radar's range(radius) in terms of both height and horizontal distance.
ii)The Radar interface that shows it's implimentation(as of right now limited to terminal but may add GUI.
iii)All 4 directions North, South, East and West.

2)Functionality:
i)We will move the mouse arrow which will act as an unknown object, when we move it into the onscree display of the radar
it will use data sctuructures/algorithms to detect the unknown object(which is the mouse key we move) and give us the Horizontal distance
height(height will always be kept constant) and displacement from us and show us the output and also detect the speed and acceleration
of the flying object.

ii)After Detection of the object since this is an air defence Radar it will also tell our air defence gun on the ground where
to set its trajectory so that it can hit the flying object. The Radar will calculate what angle from the ground the air defence
gun will face and in what direcrection to land an exact hit(for example 30 degrees north east)

iii)Friendly flying objects will also be in the radar range whose data will prolly be available and the radar will differentiate
between the unknown danger and the friendly object using data structures and algorithms.

3)Things to keep in mind:
i)Regarding the unknown flying object(mouse arrow)it speed and acceleration output will change depending on how fast we move it and it should
update in real time as we move the mouse around the designated detectation radius.

ii)It's distances and displacement from the airdefece gun must also change in real time as the object may be constantly in mothing and the 
direction the gun should point in should too as well.

iii)Setting the radius as how much space on are screen equated to how many meters of distance.


4)This that need to be displayed on the output screen:
i)Speed and Acceleration of flying object.(must keep updating in real time)
ii)Distance, height and Displacement of the flying object from the gun.(also updated in real time)
iii)Angle from the the ground and directin of the air defence gun so that it may shoot the flying object.
iv)The Radar interface(for now on terminal)
